Written offer for source code
-----------------------------
For at least three years from the date you received this copy of PiSandbox, and for as
long as we offer spare parts or support for it, anyone who has it can get the complete
corresponding source code of the GPL- and LGPL-licensed parts above (PRoot, talloc, and
the Debian packages in the root filesystem), as well as PiSandbox's own source code, on a
medium customarily used for software interchange, for no more than the cost of
physically performing the distribution. The same source is also available free of charge
from the project page shown in the app (Settings > About & licenses) and from the upstream
locations listed above. To request it, open an issue on the project page or use the
contact address given there, quoting the PiSandbox version number.
