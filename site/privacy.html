PiSandbox privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026 (applies to version 0.5.0 and later).

PiSandbox collects no data. We (the PiSandbox developers) receive nothing from the app: no personal information, no usage statistics, no device identifiers, no crash reports, no location.

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs.
  • The app itself never connects to the internet. It has no servers to talk to and no update checks. Network traffic happens only when you cause it:
    • pages you open in the Browser tab (typed addresses, bookmarks, and searches, which go to DuckDuckGo);
    • programs you run inside Linux, such as apt, pip, git or curl. They connect to the servers you or they choose (for example deb.debian.org), exactly as on a computer;
    • Linux uses your phone's DNS servers; if Android does not report any, it falls back to 1.1.1.1 and 8.8.8.8. This is only used by programs you run.
  • Browser: Android System WebView's usage-metrics upload is turned off for this app, and Safe Browsing is turned off (it would send URL hash prefixes to Google). Websites you visit can see your IP address and set cookies, as in any browser; cookies stay on the phone.
  • Crashes: if the app crashes it shows the details on your screen. They are stored only on your phone. You decide whether to copy them and send them to anyone.
  • Your files: everything in Linux (your home folder, installed packages) is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. Android's backup is not used for it.
  • Permissions: Internet and network state (for the browser and your Linux programs), and "foreground service" (to keep Linux running with a notification). No contacts, camera, microphone, location or storage permissions.
  • Children: PiSandbox is suitable for learners; since it collects nothing, there is nothing to share.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/pisandbox/pisandbox/issues (the project page is also listed in the app under Settings → About & licenses). Changes to this policy will be listed in the changelog and dated here.
